Part 1: The Venue's Identity & History

The Sessions House is a breathtaking Grade II listed former courthouse, originally built in 1843, and now stands as one of Lincolnshire's most unique exclusive-use venues. Its rich history and magnificent neo-gothic architecture provide a truly unforgettable backdrop for any special occasion, blending historic grandeur with modern elegance. We are situated in the market town of Spalding in the South of Lincolnshire on the Cambridgeshire border close to Peterborough and Stamford.



Part 2: Overview of Weddings

We specialize in creating truly unforgettable weddings. As an exclusive-use venue, the entire building and its beautiful spaces become your private stage for the day. We are fully licensed for civil ceremonies. Some highlights include our traditional Old Courtroom with rood panels, the modern Emerald Room with fully-licensed bar, the stately home-stylled magistrates room and the spacious outdoor Courtyard.



We offer a range of inhouse catering options as well as supporting external caterers subject to T&Cs.



Our dedicated team works closely with every couple to bring their unique vision to life, ensuring a day that is seamless, personal, and absolutely perfect.
